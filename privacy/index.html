Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BreakThru LLC, doing business as BreakThru Growth ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit BreakThruGrowth.com (the "Site"), download our free guides, book a call, or contact us.

Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you request a free guide, join a waitlist, apply for a program, book a call, or contact us, we collect what you provide. This may include your name, email address, phone number, business name, website, and your answers to our questions.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we and our service providers may collect limited technical information, such as your browser type, device, and pages viewed, through cookies and similar technologies. This helps the Site work and helps us understand how it is used.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to send the guides and updates you request; schedule and hold calls; review applications; respond to your questions; provide services you hire us for; operate and improve the Site; and comply with legal obligations.

Text Messages

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we may send messages about your request, appointments, and our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Site and our business, such as our customer-relationship and email provider, our scheduling and video-call tools, and our website host, and only as needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Information We Handle for Clients

When a client hires us for services such as AI tools or a reactivation campaign, we may handle information about that client's own customers. We use it only to provide the services the client asked for, under our agreement with that client. We do not sell it or use it for our own marketing. The client is responsible for having the right to share it and for any required consent.

Cookies and Affiliate Links

The Site may use cookies and similar technologies to work properly and to measure usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. If you click a link to Amazon, Amazon may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any email, and opt out of texts by replying STOP. To ask what information we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, email us at Gregg at BreakThruGrowth dot com.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide what you asked for, to serve you as a client, or as required by law.

Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for business owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above shows when it was last revised.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email us at Gregg at BreakThruGrowth dot com.
